🔹 Tamt

Arabic-first marketplace for auctions, direct sales, and tenders

A marketplace where individuals, companies, and service partners meet to sell, bid, and buy. Users can join as an individual, a company, or a partner.

Sell in multiple ways: electronic auction, hybrid auction, direct purchase, or tender. Auctions support a start price, deposit, bid step, minimum sale price, and winner award window.
Company accounts: sell under one account and manage representatives and account managers.
Service partners: publish paid services, take orders, and mark them complete.
Listings: featured placement, map pins, category-specific details, and Enforcement (Enfaz) listings with license number and brochure.
Deal flow: live bidding, Buy Now, and accept/reject/counter offers. Checkout runs through a cart grouped by seller, with card payment.
Handover: buyer and seller confirm with a delivery code, with Absher referenced where official ownership transfer is required.
Ratings: both sides rate the deal on commitment, quality, price, and treatment.
Around the sale: map search with clustered pins, filters, personalized home feed, in-app chat, push notifications, deep links, and a premium plan (Primo) for extra placement and tools.
Guest browsing before signing up with a phone number.
